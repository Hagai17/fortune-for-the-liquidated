Entry for the make.fun bounty [Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated Trader](https://make.fun/c/u-clever-harbor-75-fortune-cookie-for-the-liquidated-38fd239c?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=fortune-for-the-liquidated).

`main.py` writes the fortune to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The oracle sees green in your future: a small salad, glowing with health—all the liquidation left you.

The line was picked by duelling a dozen-plus drafts against each other and
against the five strongest entries of the live field (594 finished artifacts
read first-hand), scored in both orders under the competition's published
judge brief — a freshly liquidated trader who keeps the fortune that makes
them laugh instead of cry. The full draft list and duel record are in
[candidates.md](candidates.md).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt and prints the fortune
```
